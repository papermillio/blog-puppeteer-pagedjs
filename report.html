1   Executive Summary

Q2 2026 closed with revenue of £4.82m, up 11.4% quarter-on-quarter and 27.9% year-on-year. Gross margin held at 68.2% despite continued input-cost pressure in the European supply chain, and the customer base grew to 1,214 active accounts.

Three themes dominated the quarter. First, the self-serve channel continued to compound: it now represents 31% of new bookings, up from 22% in Q1, with materially lower acquisition cost. Second, enterprise expansion revenue exceeded plan for the fourth consecutive quarter, driven principally by seat growth in existing logistics accounts. Third, churn in the SMB cohort ticked up to 3.1% monthly — above our 2.5% threshold — concentrated in accounts acquired through the March promotional pricing.

Key figures

Net revenue retention finished the quarter at 117%, comfortably above the 110% plan. Cash conversion improved to 89% of EBITDA. Headcount grew from 84 to 91, with all seven hires in engineering and customer success. The board-approved hiring plan for H2 remains 12 additional roles, contingent on the SMB churn figure returning below threshold by end of Q3.

Management's overall assessment: the business is executing to plan on growth and unit economics, with SMB retention the single metric requiring intervention. A remediation programme is described in section 4.

Channel mix

Direct sales remains the largest channel at 54% of new bookings but is declining as a share as self-serve scales. Partner-sourced revenue was flat quarter-on-quarter at 15%; the partner programme review scheduled for September will determine whether continued investment is justified. Self-serve conversion from trial to paid improved from 8.2% to 9.7% following the onboarding redesign shipped in April.

4   Outlook & Risks

Guidance for Q3 is revenue of £5.15–5.30m, assuming SMB churn returns to threshold by September and no material FX movement. The enterprise pipeline entering the quarter is 1.4× the Q2 figure, weighted toward logistics and professional services.

SMB churn remediation

The promotional cohort from March shows a distinct usage signature: accounts that never configured a second workspace churn at four times the rate of those that did. Customer success will run a structured activation campaign against the 240 at-risk accounts through August, with a success gate reviewed at the September board meeting.

Principal risks

Concentration risk remains the most significant item on the register: the top ten accounts represent 34% of MRR. FX exposure to the euro is partially hedged through Q4. The engineering dependency on a single senior platform engineer for the billing system is being addressed through the current hiring round.
